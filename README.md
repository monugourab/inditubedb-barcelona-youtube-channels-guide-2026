## Key Aspects of Barcelona's Culture

When exploring Barcelona, focus on its architectural wonders, culinary delights, and artistic heritage. Key areas include:

- **Cuisine**: Traditional Catalan dishes and vibrant food markets.
- **Festivals**: Events like La Mercè that showcase local traditions.
- **Art**: Museums and galleries featuring works by Picasso and Miró.
